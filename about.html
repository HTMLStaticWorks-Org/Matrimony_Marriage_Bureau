Our identity

About Sutram

Sutram is built around one simple idea: two individuals can come together while keeping their own stories, values and identities.

A bond that respects individuality.

Our proposed matchmaking approach brings together personal choice, family context and thoughtful introductions. We believe trust comes from transparency—not exaggerated promises.

Our visual identity uses an intertwined knot to represent two distinct paths meeting in a shared bond. It is intentionally not tied to one community or wedding custom.

Our principles

Respect. Privacy. Clarity.

The foundations that should guide every profile, introduction and conversation.

Respect

Every person has the right to make informed choices without pressure.

Privacy

Personal information should be shared only for clear purposes and with permission.

Transparency

Verification scope, membership terms and service limitations should always be clear.
