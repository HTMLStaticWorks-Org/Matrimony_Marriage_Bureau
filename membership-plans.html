Membership questions

Clear answers before you enrol

Transparency around upgrades, privacy and support.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, members can transition from Silver Match to Gold Assisted or Royal Concierge as their needs evolve.

Are profiles public?

No. Across every tier, your profile is visible only within the authenticated Sutram environment per your settings.

How do advisors help?

Advisors help refine your profile narrative, suggest compatible matches and coordinate initial family conversations.

Can I pause my plan?

Speak with our membership desk about pausing visibility while you focus on an active introduction.
